A component whose LoRA bakes a package's overlay hook feeds (`lora.Hook`: H3's Turbo, Qwen-Image's Lightning, FLUX.2 [dev] Turbo) is neither held nor given a scope pass by its component scope: each forward is its own varena pass and declares the fill identity with the overlay armed. A scope hold filled H3's DiT blocks before its forward armed the Turbo LoRA and marked them baked, so those blocks ran base weights for the whole call (runs 19464, 19480, 19471); base and Turbo forwards also shared one fill identity, so Turbo-baked pages (and their decoded tensors) could serve a base forward. A fill seeds its rounding from the identity declared at its pass, never re-deciding the serving recipe mid-forward. Graphs no longer capture a root with an overlay hook.
