A model download that ends unfinished says why. Its `model fetch` end record carries `reason` (TensorFS's code and detail, or the cancellation), and `paused_for` names the parent request when demand paused a prefetch hint (run 2322 recorded only `completed: false`). A canceled preparation is refused as "model preparation canceled" instead of "materialization refused: TRANSFER_FAILED", and other refusals keep TensorFS's detail. Adapter pulls take their preparation's cancellation, and a machine stop cancels a job's model pulls.
