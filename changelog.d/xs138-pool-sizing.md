A recording's graph pool is sized from the working peak of the eager parts it records (`before`, the warm steps, `after`, as the core measured them), not the process's peak, which counted a load's transients: an int8 DiT's 20 GB pool no longer refuses on an A40, and Qwen-Image's 4 MP pool is 2.95 GiB, not 6.57. An `after` not yet run is bounded by the process's peak at capture, as before. A refused recording's pools and pages go with it instead of waiting for pressure (three refused SDXL calls had left 12 pools, 9 GiB mapped), and a refused first call keeps what its pool lacked for the next attempt.
