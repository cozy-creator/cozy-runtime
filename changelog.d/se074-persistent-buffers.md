A persistent buffer is checkpoint state, bound like a parameter (se-074). The serving substrate kept persistent buffers as the host tensors their construction made, so on a GPU varena bound the checkpoint's values only inside the module's forward, and a direct read (Klein's and Diffusers' Flux3 VAE `bn.running_mean`/`running_var`) returned mean 0 and var 1. Klein then skipped its latent normalization: washed-out images with an 8 px speckle grid (owner runs 14398–14403). Persistent buffers now go to `meta` at registration and become varena placeholders that read the checkpoint's value anywhere; non-persistent buffers keep their constructed values. On BFL's own example, Klein's output went from 22.6 dB to 57.4 dB against BFL's PNG.
