A Sol sparse step never waits for its kernel (cr-217). Until now, at a new video size, the first sparse step waited on the request thread for that length's Sol object to compile; H3 run 14615 stalled 436 s at step 5. Now, while the object builds, that step runs the bound dense reference, counted as `dense_building` in `attention.sol.calls`. The object serves from the step boundary after it is ready. A step never mixes the two, and `attention.sol.kernel` rows say `building` (the step it began running dense) and `built` (the step it began serving sparse). A build that failed leaves the rest dense, with a `failed` row. The mechanism is `internal/kernel_ready`: a kernel serves only once it is built and loaded off the request thread, and what lands mid-run serves from the next step or stage boundary. Each such swap is a `kernel.ready` confession with its stage and step.
