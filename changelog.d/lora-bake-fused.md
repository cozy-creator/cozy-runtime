A GPU LoRA bake is one fused Triton kernel per weight, timed by CUDA events instead of a device sync per region. Its kernels are compiled and checked on exact values in a child process started at load; the executor only loads them from Triton's cache, and a child that fails or crashes leaves it baking eagerly (`modes.lora_bake`: `fused` or `eager (<why>)`). A forward that first wants a DiT baked bakes its whole resident set before its first block; one that finds it baked for another overlay refills every stale region in one placement. Baked bytes are variant `cozy.lora-bake/2`.
