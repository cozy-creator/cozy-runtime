Idle work never makes a request wait for the device: an idle job's ops keep at most two queued there (a DiT forward queued ahead of the host made a 4090's Anima request wait 0.4 s after the yield), a warm-up that kept its host waiting half its time is device-bound without a busy measure, and a busy measure whose device falls 10 ms past its gate ends device-bound. A yield (`Yielded`, now beside `Building`) passes through cozy-int8's fallback instead of running its eager law and reporting the op failed, and an idle kernel open it interrupts leaves no verdict (it had marked the kernel failed for the process). A host page's whole zero fill is gone (only alignment gaps are zeroed) and a page filled as a command arrives registers at the next idle. Every call's wait for idle work is in its record (`idle.waited`); one of 100 ms or more is request-path blocking. The stage reads "waiting for idle work".

An idle whole-call recording whose step cannot be recorded (900-908: an RTX 3060's SDXL 1024 step frees memory inside its capture) is refused for its layout, as on a call's path, instead of poisoning the executor: since 0.36.4 that failed every second warm SDXL request on a 12 GB card.
