No call keeps a streamed program, and its replay paid for neither its recording nor the extra store reads the next request made after it: Qwen-Image int8 1 MP on an RTX 3070, warm, eager 45.6-46.8 s and 55-56 GB moved against native 49.3-50.7 s and 84-86 GB; MiniMax-H3 FL2VA 15 s on an RTX 5090, 716 s eager against 737 s native. The loop row says `"eager": "streamed, and no call keeps the program"`. A closed native sequence also drops its last execution, which referred back to it.
