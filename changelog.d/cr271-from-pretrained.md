`from_pretrained` works inside `Model.load()`: `DiffusionPipeline.from_pretrained(loader.path, torch_dtype=...)`, any Diffusers or Transformers model's `from_pretrained(loader.path, subfolder=...)`, and tokenizers, processors and schedulers. `loader.path` is the bound model's folder without its weights (`model_index.json`, configs, tokenizer and processor files), read-only. Models are built from their configs and filled through TensorFS as one construction when `load()` returns, so their memory is counted and paged as before; `torch_dtype=` is served as a held dtype, and `device_map`, `variant` and `quantization_config` are ignored with a warning. The package contract no longer refuses `.from_pretrained(...)`.
