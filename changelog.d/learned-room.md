Under an owner's process cap a stage asks its owner for room (`DeviceRoom`) before it plans when other processes leave less free than its measured activations need, instead of recovering after free memory has already dropped. Invoke may carry `floor_bytes` (the owner's physical free floor) and `activation_bytes` (per-method activation growth the owner measured for the request's shape in earlier processes; it only raises the executor's own estimates). A stage's measured activation growth counts the segments torch reserved for it, not only its tensors' peak.
