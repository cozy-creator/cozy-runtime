`vlm.write`, `vlm.ask` and so every caption stream their answers as they are made: each answer's words go to the run's followers as its text output's deltas (`response.output_text.delta` on the machine), the thought held back (the stage says `thinking`), and the result is unchanged. The engine under `vlm` hands over its text through `on_delta` and returns a `Final`. `TextBox` and `AskRequest` take `reasoning_effort`, passed to a chat template that reads it (Qwen3.8's).
