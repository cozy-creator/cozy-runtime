A construction's LoRAs are merged ahead of its first request (cl-1318): `map`, at either level, first fills each LoRA'd component's blocks once on the GPU under the fill its request will ask, its declared LoRAs and an overlay's arming read off the loaded models (the one other model of the construction whose factor modules sit at the hooked paths: H3's Turbo). The merged pages stay in the host tier and go to TensorFS's temporary-storage cache, flushed before a `ram` level lets the tier go. A call that comes meanwhile waits for `map` and merges nothing again. A record's `moved.bake.factors_ns` is the part of its bakes' host time spent binding another model's factors.
