On a GPU that also drives the computer's display, a call pauses for the desktop to draw: once `work_ms` has passed since its last pause, at a block's entry or an eager step's end, it waits for the GPU and sleeps `pause_ms` (80 ms and 16 ms by default, `[machine.display_pace]`), keeping about 83% of its speed. The machine sends the pace in Start only when the session's GPU drives a display on a shared machine; an older machine sends none and nothing pauses. A graph capture and a native resident program are never paused.
