Each card tunes its own 8-bit GEMMs (xs-065, after magnitude). A served call's prebuilt int8 or fp8 GEMM runs the kernel's rule (`choose()`) until this card has measured better for that exact shape: while an executor is idle and no other process on the GPU runs a request, the shapes its calls ran are timed on the family's configs (interleaved rounds behind a sleep, operands over twice the L2), and a config serves only if its output is bit-identical to the rule's on the same operands, its rate is one the card can reach, and it beats the rule by more than both spreads and 1%. At most 60 s per executor; a command waits only for the launch group in flight. Measurements are kept on the machine (`<kernel store>/tuned/`, keyed by the kernel binary, card and driver); a shape that could not be measured is remembered in that process only; nothing is uploaded, and a missing or stale table is the rule. A run says `gemm.tuned`: shapes measured, faster, waiting, and the median gain.
