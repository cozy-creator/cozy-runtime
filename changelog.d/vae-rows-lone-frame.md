`cozy_runtime.tiles.rows(fn, x, halo=, scale=)`: a row-local block over the whole map in strips of rows, each reading a halo either side and keeping its own rows, so the result is the block's on the whole map with a strip's working set (2^25 elements). An image VAE decodes the whole canvas exactly this way instead of in blended tiles, as ComfyUI does. `cozy_runtime.fusion.LoneFrame` / `install_lone_frame`: a video VAE's causal convolution on a lone frame with no frame cache runs as one 2-D convolution on its last temporal tap, with the spatial zero padding inside the convolution. Diffusers instead pads two zero frames, convolves all 27 taps and copies the padded input. The VAE's nearest upsampler runs in the activations' dtype, with the same bits as Diffusers' fp32 round trip.
