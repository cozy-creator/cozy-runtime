A Ulysses group on the copy engines no longer stalls when a request is longer than the last one (run 13108, segment 2). The exchange's receive buffer is mapped once as the group forms, an exchange larger than it crosses in rounds, and its barriers are NCCL collectives, so a GPU that fails mid-exchange ends its peers' wait instead of leaving them in a rendezvous or a spin. The group no longer judges its followers on a clock (the 30 s patience a call with no history got): each wait ends with the reply or the follower's death, and TensorD judges a stalled call from outside on its GPUs' memory traffic, bytes, CPU and frames. A follower failure after GPU 0's own names GPU 0's first.
