A group measures its GPUs' links once per boot (verified direct copies against pinned-host staging, then a group of its own trampolined processes running NCCL over peer memory and a copy-engine exchange through symmetric memory), sets NCCL's peer level from that, and where every peer copy lands correctly times a copy-engine Ulysses all-to-all against NCCL at formation and keeps the faster. Each call's execution record carries the route (`transport`), shown by `cozy run show`. On 4x H100 NVL with NVLink only within pairs one H3 attention exchange took 362 ms through NCCL and 55 ms on the copy engines; hosts whose peer copies are slow or corrupt keep NCCL with peer memory on NVLink only (cozy-runtime#1297).
