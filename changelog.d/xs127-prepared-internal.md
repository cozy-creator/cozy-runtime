Hard cut: `cozy_runtime.compute` is gone. `PreparedRepeat`/`PreparedSequence` are internal (`cozy_runtime.internal.prepared`), driven only by `author.denoise_loop`; `ctx.plan` takes component steps only; `author.Execution`, `PreparedWork` and `Progress` are no longer exported; the unused internal raw-stage helper and the entrypoint `prepare=` factory (program warming) are deleted. Executors no longer announce `program_preparation`, so TensorD warms their models without a payload.
