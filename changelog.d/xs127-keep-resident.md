Weights that fit stay resident across warm requests. A component phase whose weights all fit beside the other phases' idle ones reserves no streaming window: on a 24 GB card Krea 2 Turbo's text encoder (one 4.3 GB block) reserved a 13 GB window that took 3.9 GB of the idle DiT on every request. Each phase change returns the last phase's unused Torch cache, a pure cut site counts Torch's cached bytes as room (a step beside resident weights no longer empties the cache at every site: Krea 2 Turbo's 2048² DiT stepped 14% slower that way), and a weight fill returns Torch's cache before evicting another phase's weights (Krea 2's cold fill evicted its text encoder; SDXL's cold UNet on 8 GB streamed). Krea 2 Turbo 2048² warm on a 4090: 28.0 → 24.0 s. Requires TensorFS >= 0.17.6, whose allocator hook lets Torch free its cache before Varena evicts weights.
