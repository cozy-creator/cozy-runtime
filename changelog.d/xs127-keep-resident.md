Weights that fit stay resident across warm requests. A component phase whose weights all fit reserves no streaming window, and each phase returns the last one's unused Torch cache before its activations can evict idle weights. On a 24 GB card Krea 2 Turbo at 2048² lost 7.4 GB of its DiT per warm request: 3.9 GB to the text encoder's 13 GB window, 3.5 GB to the VAE decode beside 4.8 GB of the DiT's unused activations.
