Weights that fit stay resident across warm requests. A component phase whose weights all fit reserves no streaming window, and each phase returns the last one's unused Torch cache before its activations can evict idle weights. A pure cut site counts Torch's cached bytes as room, so a step beside resident weights no longer empties the cache (a device sync) at every site: Krea 2 Turbo's 2048² DiT stepped 14% slower on a 24 GB card that way.
