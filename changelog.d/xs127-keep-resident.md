Weights that fit stay resident across warm requests. A component phase whose weights all fit beside the other phases' idle ones reserves no streaming window: on a 24 GB card Krea 2 Turbo's text encoder (one 4.3 GB block) reserved a 13 GB window that took 3.9 GB of the idle DiT on every request. Each phase change returns the last phase's unused Torch cache, and a pure cut site counts Torch's cached bytes as room, so a step beside resident weights no longer empties the cache (a device sync) at every site: Krea 2 Turbo's 2048² DiT stepped 14% slower that way. Needs TensorFS >= 0.17.6, whose allocator hook lets Torch free its cache before Varena evicts weights.
