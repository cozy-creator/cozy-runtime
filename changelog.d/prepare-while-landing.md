A Host may start a published package's preparation while it is still landing the models (`PreparePackageSetRequest.models_landing`, reported by `ProtocolInfoResult.supports_prepare_while_landing`, h3a-089). Runtime installs the release, reads its interface and answers the installation without reading a model, and starts that installation's executor on free GPUs, proving its attention kernels, before any root exists. The first root that asks for the installation, or is granted one of those GPUs for it, adopts the process; its `Starting model executor` and `Proving attention kernels` rows reach that root's journal with the times they ran. A process no root adopted yields its GPUs to any other root, and to the collection of its installation. The Host's second preparation, after every model is verified in the Store, authors the PlacementSet as before.
