Sibling encoded leaves share one quantization of their input without a scope: int8, w4a8 and fp8 leaves that read the same unchanged tensor in a row (q, k and v of one normed input, an MLP's gate and up, a text encoder's projections) quantize it once (`kernels.shared_operand`), for every model. The operand is held only until the maker's learned group has read it, another input is quantized or the input is freed: one at most, never a stale one (version, storage and geometry checked), never across a graph capture, and not for inference tensors (no version counter). `quantize_once` scopes still take precedence. MiniMax H3's own attention drops two of three quantizations per call, bit-identical (xs-089).
