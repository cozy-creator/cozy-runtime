A stage that squeezes inside one entry (H3's whole denoise is one) pages out an idle component at its next block boundary, once torch has dropped its whole cache more than once since the entry or the last page-out; page-outs are in the plane's facts (`paged_out`, `paged_out_bytes`). An executor's room request carries what it holds now (`DeviceRoom.holding_bytes`), so the machine does not count its torch memory as external when it raises the cap.
