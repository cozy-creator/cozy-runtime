The int8 quantize prologue's SwiGLU also takes one fused projection's rows: `kernels.int8_rows(x, (down,), activation="swiglu")` with no `up` reads each `[..., 2F]` row as its [up | gate] halves (Diffusers' `SwiGLU`), as SiTU reads its own, so a down projection after a fused gate-and-up linear quantizes `silu(gate) * up` itself and the bf16 product is never written. MiniMax H3's FFN is that layout: one [109k, 14336] bf16 write and read less per block at 15 s (xs-089). The probe checks the halves form against the law in bf16 and fp16.
