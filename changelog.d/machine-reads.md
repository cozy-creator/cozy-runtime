The machine answers for itself (wire 66): `ListMachineExecutions` lists its runs by `number` (oldest or newest first, by state, with a long poll), `ListPackages` its installations, `ListModels` its TensorFS store's rows and usage, and `DescribeMachine` this Runtime's environment. Runs are numbered at acceptance; roots an older Runtime accepted are numbered when the journal next opens, with no schema version change. An acknowledgement means delivered: with a TensorFS that tracks delivered tree roots, products stay until its GC evicts them under pressure; an older TensorFS releases them at the acknowledgement, as before.
