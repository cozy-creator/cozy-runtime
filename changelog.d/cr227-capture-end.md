A `denoise_loop` recording whose capture fails ends that capture before the loop runs on eagerly (cr-227). The loop refuses a CUDA capture error (900–908) and continues eagerly, but `_Graph.end` recorded its join before `capture_end`: on an invalidated capture the join raised, the end was skipped and both recording streams stayed capturing, so the eager rest failed `device_fault` (900) and the idle thread's next legacy-stream call 906. SDXL's first warm call after a cold one on an RTX 5090 hit it (Runtime 0.35.8), and every step that cannot be recorded did (a host read such as `.item()`, comfy-kitchen's int8 path). The graph domain's owner capture ends the same way, and a poisoned executor takes no more idle work.
