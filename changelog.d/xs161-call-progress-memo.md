A managed call is written at the call site: `await call(fn, ...)` takes `fn`'s own arguments less the injected ones and returns its result; a plain `fn(...)` is an ordinary Python call. `@invocable` is gone, its options live on `app.entrypoint`, and another package's functions are no longer swapped for generated proxies. A function's request is its one struct parameter, or else its plain parameters. Progress is one call, `ctx.progress(step, total)`, under the enclosing `tel.stage`; `tel.step_callback`, `tel.progress` and `overall_range=` are gone, and the machine places the overall bar from its own time estimate. `memoize=True` is the only memo option: a request that leaves an optional `Seed` unset runs without the cache (`Memoize` is gone).
