An executor drops the constructions TensorD names in a `release` (tfs-303; its hello says `releases: 1`), each that no call is prepared or running on and no load is building, while calls on its other constructions run: the construction leaves at once, so a later `load` builds it anew, and on a thread of its own its models leave the memory (regions, host pages, graphs) and the TensorFS checkpoints it opened close, so this process no longer holds those models in the store or their chunk files open, and the machine can evict them. A GPU group ignores it.
