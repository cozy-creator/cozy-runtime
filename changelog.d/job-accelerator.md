`app.job(accelerator=False)` declares a pure-CPU job and `accelerator=True` a job that computes on an accelerator. The declaration rides the job's package-interface entry, so a host places a CPU conversion on a CPU machine even when the package's dependencies include torch. An undeclared job writes no field and leaves the class to the host. The builtin `quantize` and `prepare_model` operations declare CPU.
