A group of GPUs serves a kernel built during a run from its next sharded call, as one GPU does from its next step: H3 over two Hopper GPUs ran every sparse step dense, because Sol's per-length kernel landed mid-segment and a group only switched kernels between segments. The copy-engine exchange now maps inside an executor (its buffer comes straight from symmetric memory's allocator; varena's refused the MemPool `symm.empty` opens), so peer-capable PCIe pairs no longer fall back to NCCL.
