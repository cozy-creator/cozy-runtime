An LM call's decode memory is no longer refused from a report of free room. `lm` refuses up front only slots larger than the whole card less the model's own weights in use; otherwise it allocates them, and the allocation asks idle executors for their memory, evicts and waits as every other one does. A refusal after all of that is the call's `does_not_fit`. Before, a prompt upsampler that needed 0.6 GiB failed with "this card has 0.1 GiB" while idle executors of other packages held the rest.
