A buffer a module derives from a constant parameter while it is built has its value: such a parameter (`requires_grad=False`, at most 1 MiB, e.g. rotary_embedding_torch's frequencies) keeps its value until its factory returns, then goes weightless like every parameter. SeedVR2's F32 rotary frequencies, a non-persistent buffer, had been an unbound placeholder that refused its first read in a DiT block. A buffer no checkpoint stores that still has no value is named in a warning at install, and a read of one says why it has none.
