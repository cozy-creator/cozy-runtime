The executor's requests to TensorD (child calls and their polls, cancels and forgets, child events, GPU release, model prefetch, publish, checkpoint item, stage memo lookup and store) are built as `tensorfs.executor`'s classes and TensorD's answers decoded by `tensorfs.executor.decode_answer`; the Runtime's own request and answer structs, its grant structs and the `respond`/`decode` side nothing used are gone. Requires tensorfs 0.15.1.
