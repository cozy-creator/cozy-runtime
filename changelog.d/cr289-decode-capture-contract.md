A prompt LM or VLM's decode is captured when Transformers declares its class's forward one compiled graph (`_can_compile_fullgraph`), not when its class is on a list (cr-289). Qwen3-VL (fibo-2's two VLMs, ideogram-4's caption LM) and Mistral3 (flux-2's upsampler) decoded every token eagerly ("no shipped decode capture contract"): fibo-2's edit rewriter took about 50 s for ~1,000 tokens on an RTX 3090.
