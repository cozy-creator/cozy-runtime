Media inputs are handles read by spec (cr-197). `ImageAsset`, `VideoAsset` and `AudioAsset` are the only media types a signature names, as a field or in `Assets[...]`; naming one grants `media_decode`. `Assets[Image]`, `Assets[Mixed]`, `Mixed` and the `MediaDecoder` parameter are removed: call `image.read()`, `video.read(end=, fps=, audio=)`, `video.stream(end=, audio=, pictures=)`, `audio.read(end=)`/`stream(end=)` and `probe()` on the handle (`stream_audio(video)` is `video.stream(pictures=False)`). Each frame shows until the next starts, so B-frame and variable-rate files no longer refuse `video_discontinuity`. `end` (counted from the first decoded frame, with or without pictures) bounds the decode: later frames are never validated or converted, the demux stops, the last frame is clipped and the soundtrack ends on the exact sample, so a 2-minute 1080p source reads its first 15 s instead of refusing; the 3600-frame and sample ceilings count what a read keeps, and streams have none. A refused read returns its bytes to the attempt budget. `fps=24` maps frames onto a constant clock the way minimax-h3's `_video_at_24fps` did, repeating frames by reference. `VideoPreparation`/`AudioPreparation(min_seconds=, max_seconds=, excess="refuse"|"truncate")` annotate a field or an `Assets` kind, and `ImagePreparation` now also annotates a single field: hydration probes the input and refuses `media_too_short`/`media_too_long`, or truncates every read to `max_seconds` with an `adjusted` row (`source="policy"`) and a warning. The decoded-byte budget charges retained values only and the ceiling is 16 GiB per read and per attempt; every image/video/audio request field declares `AssetBound(max_decoded_bytes=)` (`Assets` kinds default to the ceiling), and a video or audio input requires `cozy-runtime[media]`. Preparations annotate inputs, including a direct invocable parameter; on a result they refuse at describe, and every reader (describe and both static readers) applies the same placement and decoded-bound rules. The interface drops `assets.view`; kinds and field `asset_bound` carry `prepare` profiles `image-fit/1`, `video-trim/1`, `audio-trim/1` with exactly their members and a matching kind, and unknown profiles are ignored. Executor reply adjustment rows are `{field, kind, requested, applied, reason, source}`, no longer folding the kind into the reason. `fakes.fake_media_decoder` is replaced by `fakes.file_input`, `fakes.granted_input` and an attempt-bound `fakes.fake_input(..., attempt=, preparation=)`.
