Attention cells hold only kernels of their own precision (`Candidate.level`), checked where the tables are built, and a sparse site walks its sparse cells at its level and above before any dense one. Ada's (and RTX Blackwell's) 8-bit sparse cell is now Kitchen INT8 Sol, ahead of BF16 Sol: MiniMax-H3 Turbo 15 s on an RTX 4090 runs 314.6-317.1 s instead of 370.6-398.8 s (sparse steps 23.5-23.9 s instead of 33.1-36.3 s; dense steps 47.4 s instead of 52.3-55.5 s, the exact rows taking Kitchen INT8 as the route declares). tcgen05's sparse 8-bit cell is empty and steps up to Sol. Every other family walks as before. On a quantizing sparse route (Kitchen's), a layer whose key RMSNorm gain has outlier channels runs dense on its exact kernel at every step: H3 block 49 on an RTX 4090 errs 0.016-0.022 rel L2 against FP32 that way, against 0.10-0.11 on Kitchen INT8 Sol and 0.05-0.08 on BF16 Sol.
