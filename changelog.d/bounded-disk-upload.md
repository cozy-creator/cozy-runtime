`upload_huggingface` and `upload_civitai` download, convert and publish one checkpoint within whatever disk the machine has: each pass is admitted against the observed free disk, lands a window of source members and converts what they unlock, and the new output is put in Hub custody through a progress publication before the next pass, so GC may drop it. Nothing is evicted until no pass that makes progress fits: custodied output first, then spent source, then the workspace cache. The TensorFS journal and the Hub's verified objects are the durable progress, so an interrupted upload resumes without converting or uploading committed work again; a machine with room keeps the source as cache for its next conversion. They replace `ingest_huggingface` + `convert_cozytensors` + `upload_checkpoint` in the ingest script. `storage_admission.acquire(recover=False)` observes a refusal without reclaiming, and admission's reclaimer also releases completed uploads' source caches.
