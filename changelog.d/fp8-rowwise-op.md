`fp8-rowwise/1` is one native op, `torch.ops.cozy.fp8_linear`, in the 8-bit library compiled at boot (recipe `cozy-int8`). One kernel quantizes each activation row to E4M3, matching the eager law bit for bit. A CUTLASS GEMM then applies both row scales and the bias in its epilogue: wgmma on Hopper, tcgen05 on B200 and B300, sm120's TMA mma on RTX 50, and cuBLASLt `_scaled_mm` on sm89. The Triton quantizer is removed; torch ops serve until the op lands. A run whose card cannot build the library warns `8-bit op unavailable, slower kernels served`.
