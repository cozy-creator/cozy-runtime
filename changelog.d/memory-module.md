One module, `internal/memory.py`, now decides device memory: the Worker's tenant manager, the executor's component residency and block paging moved into it. An out-of-memory error inside a component's forward is recovered in place: the failed step's allocations are dropped, the plane descends one rung (room from idle tenants, idle components, block paging chosen at run time, a split batch, a tiled or sliced decode) and runs that forward again. The attempt fails `device_out_of_memory` only when the lowest rung cannot fit, and the executor is not poisoned. Paging now covers nested no-split classes (SDXL's UNet) and pages parameters only. A machine may cap the Runtime's GPU memory with `gpu.budget` in `etc/cozy/runtime.yaml` under its root; idle executor processes are ended when their contexts are what stands in the way.
