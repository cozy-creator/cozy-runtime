Video interfaces (xs-136). `ShotRequest`/`ShotResult` (`video/shot`: `prompt`, `duration_s`, `beats` of `Beat(at, prompt)`, `seed`, `style`, `assets` of `ShotAsset`, `world_state` of `WorldState(name, image, layout)`, `writes`) and `UpscaleRequest`/`UpscaleResult` (`video/upscale`) join the opt-in author library; a package extends them. `app.entrypoint(..., implements=Implements("video/shot", max_piece_s=15))` declares the interface an entrypoint implements; the interface document and the static reader carry it as `implements`.
