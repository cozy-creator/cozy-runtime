The baked-page cache measures its room as the tightest cgroup on this process's path, not the host's `MemAvailable`, which a container reports for the whole host. It keeps a page only into room beyond 8 GiB and evicts nothing to make room: blocks refill in a fixed order, so replacing the least recently used page missed every refill once the cache was full. Each refill gives pages back, least recently used first, while host RAM is under the headroom. A resident block's baked bytes are kept from its first refill, so a prefix that is displaced once per call stops re-baking. `baked_pages.room` reports the room at the cache's last look.
