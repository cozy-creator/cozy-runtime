An executor that cannot fit a load, activation or component admission by evicting its own components asks the worker for room (`device_room`), and the worker frees idle co-tenants of its devices LRU-first, allocator caches before weights, before anything refuses. A load's pre-check counts a construction's weights once, not once per binding. A warm refused on device capacity is typed `device_shortfall`/`device_out_of_memory` and never marks its binding failed, so the next request prepares afresh instead of `prepare refused: every binding`.
