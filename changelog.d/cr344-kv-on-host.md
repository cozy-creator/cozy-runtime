An LM call whose keys and values would outgrow the card's room keeps them on the host: Transformers' loop runs it with its offloaded cache (each attention layer's keys come to the card for its own attention, the next layer's on their way meanwhile), decided from the room the card has when the call starts, and slots larger than the whole card go to that loop instead of being refused. At a 6 GiB budget a 130,000-token prompt on Qwen3.8-27B needs 8.5 GB of keys and values (cr-344).
