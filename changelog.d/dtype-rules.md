One dtype rule: a component serves its stored dtype, or the dtype `hold_dtype` names. Fit pins every built dtype exactly (bf16 and f16 included), as the fill always has, and asks consent only for tensors the plan serves through a native encoded leaf, as materialize does (cl-681). A placed module changed by `module.to(dtype)`, `.half()` or `.float()` refuses at its next bind, naming `hold_dtype`, where it used to cast every weight on every forward outside varena's accounting; a CPU executor still widens to float32. A float16 hold refuses a stored value that would round to ±inf (TensorFS's import narrowing rule), and a hold on a CPU executor is a no-op.
