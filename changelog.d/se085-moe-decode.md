`cozy_runtime.moe` decodes a few rows (decode, a verify pass) as one grouped GEMV per projection over every (token, expert) pair, each expert's weight read through an address table (bf16 and int8.convrot), so routing stays on the device; `moe.install` builds it from `Model.execution_plan`. lm decodes a mixture of experts whose experts are split (`moe.Experts`) in its own loop, drafted and graph-captured like a dense model; fused experts keep Transformers' loop.
