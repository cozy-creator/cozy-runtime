The dev-only attention capture (`cozy run --attention-capture`) sees every call: each attention module is named, including a fused block's composed attention and VAE blocks; a producer's site serves through its base Sol kernel, so sparse steps are counted and captured with their layout; a kernel's nested calls (Sol's dense rows) belong to their site call; no CUDA graph replays or records while capturing; and each row carries the Runtime's own `stage` and `step` from `ctx.progress`.
`cozy_runtime.bench`'s attention speed and quality runs count their grid points under the stage "measure", so a gate never reads them as sampler steps.
