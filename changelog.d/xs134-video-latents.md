Video interfaces carry a shot's latents (xs-134 H). `ShotResult.latents` and `UpscaleRequest.latents`, both optional (`File`, `application/octet-stream`, at most 1 GiB, the renderer's own format): a project keeps a shot's latents with its video and hands them to its upscale, so an upscaler of the same model starts from the shot's real latent instead of re-encoding the video (minimax-h3's Regenerate: 44 s of encode at 5 s, 318 s at 40 s). Without them, or for an upscaler that cannot read them, nothing changes.
