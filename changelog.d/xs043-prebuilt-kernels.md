Prebuilt kernels (xs-043 K). Runtime depends on `cozy-kernels` (one wheel per OS and CPU: Linux x86_64 and aarch64, Windows x86_64; every GPU target's cubins plus PTX; nanobind on the stable ABI, no torch ABI). Its 8-bit op (int8/fp8 GEMMs, quantize, GEMV) and FlashAttention-3's forward serve once they pass `verify_op` on the card: a probe against the float64 law, kept per machine by (kernel, artifact, arch, driver); a kernel that fails, or ends its process, is refused, and the run says so once. Every compiled attention kernel is verified the same way. The driver leaves every compile key. Deleted: the boot-compiled 8-bit torch op (`int8_op/op.cpp`, `setup.py`, recipe `cozy-int8`), its boot build and family inheritance by build (§2.6 now takes the first family whose prebuilt kernels verify), and the trimmed FA3 recipe with its text patches (a Hopper card whose prebuilt FA3 fails builds `flash-attn3-full`).
