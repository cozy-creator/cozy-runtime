A model executor's device context starts only with room for it. Idle tenants of its GPU give room first; beside a call in flight on a full GPU it waits until that call is past its stages, so a computing call is never squeezed. A start or load that runs out of device memory before its request has run is tried again in a fresh process, after the calls ahead of it leave and every idle tenant is cut, and nothing is latched against its placement. A stage whose activations were never measured gets no grant: every idle tenant gives room and the executor derives its own budget. A request that still does not fit with every idle byte of its GPUs given (at its load, or at its turn's budget) ends FAILED with what did not fit, never REFUSED for size. When an executor asks for room mid-call, a tenant busy outside its turn (a load, an attempt's tail) gives it at its next block boundary through its budget cell; a tenant in its turn keeps its room. An idle executor stays warm while there is room: when a measured need is still short after every idle weight byte was cut, idle processes end, least recently used first, for the device contexts they hold, and restart at their next grant.
