A model executor's device context starts only with room for it. Idle tenants of its GPU give room first; beside a call in flight on a full GPU it waits until that call is past its stages, so a computing call is never squeezed. A start or load that runs out of device memory before its request has run is tried again in a fresh process, after the calls ahead of it leave and every idle tenant is cut, and nothing is latched against its placement. A stage whose activations were never measured gets no grant: every idle tenant gives room and the executor derives its own budget. A request whose load still does not fit alone on a GPU with every idle byte given ends FAILED with what did not fit, never REFUSED for size.
