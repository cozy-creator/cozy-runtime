The streaming MP4 writer keeps up with H3's decode across four ranks. The caller converts RGB to YUV with swscale on the x264 thread count and encodes the AAC; one encode thread runs x264 and every mux in submission order, so the container bytes are unchanged. On run 1436's frames, paced like its decode, the writer's rate rose from ~55 to ~88 fps and the tail after the last chunk fell from 1.2 s to 0.6 s. On the pod that tail was 3.9-4.4 s (runs 1435-1441).
