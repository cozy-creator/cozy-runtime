A warm request starts as roomy as the first. Each phase learns, in this process, the activation bytes its calls needed beside its weights; a later call's weights leave that free (stage entry streams the phase's own resident tail once, and the retained prefix stops there) instead of the activations fighting resident weights at every call. A cut site whose need Torch's own cached blocks can serve no longer reclaims first: that reclaim emptied the cache and synced the device at every such site (H3 Turbo on one RTX 5090: warm calls 22% slower than the cold one, with 169–174 s of GPU idle in allocation frames).
