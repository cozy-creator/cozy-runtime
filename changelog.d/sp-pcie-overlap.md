Sequence parallelism over PCIe without peer access aims to beat one GPU again. On 2× RTX 4090, H3 at degree 2 took 15 s a step against degree 1's 9 s: crossing 541 MB a layer each way, that is NCCL's shared-memory kernels at about 2.6 GB/s, where this pod's copy engines stage through host memory at 11 GB/s. A group whose GPU pairs are not all direct now times a copy-engine all-to-all through pinned host memory against NCCL as it forms, and keeps the faster. Every Ulysses exchange large enough crosses its heads in at least four groups, the next group's Q/K/V and the last group's output in flight while a group attends, on the exchange's own streams (and NCCL's) at high priority. An attention call's two store rounds are one, three store calls a GPU. Each call's `transport.exchange` in `cozy run show` reports its exchanges: calls, GB crossed, device ms, the attention inside, and the rest exposed.
