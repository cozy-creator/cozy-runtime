Named references and `<name>` mentions (xs-135), an opt-in author convention: `Reference` (a `name` and an open `kind`; subclasses add media and fields), `ImageReference` (`image`, `box`), and one mention parser (`name_key`, `mentions`, `render` with a package's label for the Nth reference). `ImageRequest.references` replaces `reference_images` and `reference_boxes` (deleted, with `ReferenceImages`); `rendered()` gives the prompt in the model's own words, and the static reader extends both types. Nothing below the author library knows the convention.
