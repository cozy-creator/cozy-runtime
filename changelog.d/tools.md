A tool is a struct plus a function (tracker `design/tools.md`, a hard cut). `app.tool(label=, named=, reserved=, ui=)` takes `app.entrypoint`'s options and registers a box; on a model's own function it is the shortcut. New value types: `Prompt(text, references)` and `Prompt[Image]`, `Image`, `Video` and `Audio` of `(file, description)`, `Pickup(take, out_frame)`, `Latents`, and `AspectRatio` and `Seed` as their own types. New markers, each a `msgspec.Meta`: `Range`, `Count`, `Total`, `Ratios`, `Snap`, `Spans`, `Label` (options with words, or words and a description), `Placeholder` and `Advanced`. A prompt's references travel as a list, each value with its `key`, and a file's input path is its position there (`summary.references.0.file`). The static reader emits `tool` and each entry point's `calls` (its own `@invocable`s and other packages' functions, read at the locked release, with `package`, `function`, `accelerator`, `gpus` and `models`), the schema forms `prompt`, `media`, `pickup`, `{"input": "aspect_ratio"}`, `{"input": "seed"}` and `{"asset": "latents"}`, an optional field's markers, and defaults at any depth. Publish refuses a tool's required field a box can't draw, two shapes or one in a group, a group holding itself, a result with nothing to show, and an imported cozy package `uv.lock` doesn't lock. `Node`, `Reference`, `Picture`, `PlacedPicture`, `PictureRequest`, `Plate`, `ContinueFrom`, `ShotResult`, `UpscaleResult`, `Follow`, `Preset`, `mentions`, `render` and `name_label` are gone, and `cozy_runtime.author.Image` is the struct, not PIL's.
