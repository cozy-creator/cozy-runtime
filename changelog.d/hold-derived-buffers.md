`hold_dtype` works on a component whose module has buffers the checkpoint does not store, such as H3 VAE's rotary `inv_freq`: those buffers keep their constructed dtype, as building the module in that dtype leaves them. A floating parameter the checkpoint does not store still refuses, naming it and the remedy. A hold over a lane that stores the component encoded (H3's int8 VAE) now refuses with the remedy too: hold it on a plain lane, or leave it unheld.
