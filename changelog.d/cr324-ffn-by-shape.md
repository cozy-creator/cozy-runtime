A feed-forward is recognized by its shape, whoever wrote it (cr-324): a module of linears and elementwise activations alone, up projections from its input's width (one, or gated pairs) and one down projection, whose forward names nothing that mixes tokens, cuts by tokens when the card is short, as Diffusers' FFNs do. RelayVSR's Wan FFN (package code) ran out of memory at a 6 GiB cap on its [tokens, 14336] projection; it now cuts with no hint. A package's own forward runs once (no retry), and its pieces keep its input's rank.
