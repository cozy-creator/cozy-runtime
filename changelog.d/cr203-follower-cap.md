Fixes from the 0.28.1 memory-torture gate (runs 11275, 11399, 11340, 11405). A group follower never takes a process cap below what it holds plus the GPU's free bytes above the floor: TensorD 0.10.0 counts a follower's process at its last reply (11399: a 670 MB cap while it held 7.07 GiB), so its caps counted its bytes twice; below the floor (the watchdog) a cap still binds. A cap written into a budget cell applies as soon as a room ask returns. A package that raises its own error from a package call's failure (`raise ... from failure`) fails the attempt without poisoning the executor. The memory refusal judges its verdict against the cap as well as the GPU and reports the GPU as the op found it. A failed attempt gives back what it took: its tracebacks are cleared along the cause chain, its caches and reservations shed, cycles collected, host copies swept and torch's cache emptied; bytes still allocated above the attempt's start recycle the executor (11340 kept 7.27 GiB that 11405 needed).
