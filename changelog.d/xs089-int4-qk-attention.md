INT4 Q·Kᵀ attention is the 4-bit attention of Ampere and Ada (owner 10-31, after an 8-bit vs 4-bit A/B: "I can't see any difference"). `_kernels._attn4` is comfy-kitchen's INT8 attention kernel at its int4 Q/K instantiation (s4 mma.sync m16n8k64), its V, P and P·V path unchanged; Q and K are Hadamard-turned, K centred, at the kernel's per-thread int4 scales. `kitchen-int4qk` and its Sol form `kitchen-sol-int4qk` (dense steps int4-QK, sparse steps and exact paths Kitchen INT8) fill the level-4 cells of the int4 rows (`arch.Row.int4`), after Sage3 FP4 on FP4 rows. **A model that declares 4-bit attention changes output once on Ada and Ampere:** H3 (`attention_bits` 4) now serves int4-QK there, about 1.10x end to end on H3 Turbo 15 s (RTX 4090); `--attention-kernel` with an 8-bit kernel keeps the old output. Models declaring 8 or 16 bits, and every other card, are unchanged. A wheel-only attention kernel (no source recipe) is refused as absent where the wheel lacks it.
