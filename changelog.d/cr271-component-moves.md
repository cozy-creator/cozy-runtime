Stock Diffusers and Transformers moves work on a component: `pipe.to("cuda")`, `vae.to(torch.float32)`, `.half()`, `.float()`, `.cuda()` and `.cpu()` on a placed component ignore the device and serve the dtype through the Runtime's cast (as `hold_dtype`); in `load()` a tensor move or a tensor made on a device stays on the host. `enable_model_cpu_offload`, `enable_sequential_cpu_offload`, `enable_group_offload` and `enable_layerwise_casting` do nothing: the Runtime already pages every component. A Model that declares no component use may build any component.
