A run's attention record names the hardware class its kernels were chosen for (`attention.class`): `mma.sync`, `mma.sync/half`, `mma.sync+fp4`, `mma.sync+fp4/half`, `wgmma` or `tcgen05`. Half-rate FP32 accumulation (GeForce and TITAN cards) is read from NVML's brand, since one compute capability holds both kinds (the RTX 4090 and the L40S are both sm89); a group takes the half-rate class if any of its cards has it. Selection is unchanged.
