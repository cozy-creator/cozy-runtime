The Runtime reads a GPU's power limit and MIG mode from NVML by the UUID torch names for the card it drives, never by torch's device number (cr-326): an executor sealed to `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=2,3` read cards 0 and 1. A card NVML can't find by its UUID is recorded `unmapped`. A seal entry that is an index reaches NVML only where `CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID` makes it nvidia-smi's number (an allowlisted seal name now). Each GPU of a run's execution record also says its `name` and `settings` (`300.00W/Disabled`).
