An LM prompt's prefill is a cut site (`catch.cut`): its decoder, after any picture's features are merged into its embeddings, runs the prompt in 2^level pieces of 64-token chunks a row at the level native selection takes from each piece's need, one level finer after a refusal, each piece attending to the keys and carrying the linear-attention state of those before it, in the engine and in Transformers' loop alike; and the gated-delta rule's own pieces are sized by all their rows and the layer's widths, within 256 MiB a copy, instead of 4,096 tokens a row. At a 6 GiB budget a 65,000-character prompt on Qwen3.8-27B (its norm asked 2.5 GiB) and P-H3-Image's hardest expansion (a gated norm asked 0.6 GiB) ran out of memory; uncapped, 16 prompts on Qwen3.8-27B asked 1.5 GiB for one tensor of a 65,536-token piece (cr-344).
