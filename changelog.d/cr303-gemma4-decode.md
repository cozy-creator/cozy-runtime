The decode engine runs Gemma 4: its sliding-window layers keep their keys like full attention's and take a mask that closes keys more than the window behind (Transformers' masks by layer type, which the model takes as given), its KV-shared layers read the keys their source layer returns, and each attention layer's cache is sized from its own module (Gemma 4's heads and widths differ by layer type). Gemma 4 no longer decodes in Transformers' eager loop.
