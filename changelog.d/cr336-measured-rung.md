An LM decode step's graph is chosen by measurement, not a guessed 2.5 GiB (cr-336). A step key's first step runs eagerly with nothing leased and is measured; its graph is admitted only where the weights it reads, its pool, what is live, one more eager step and its check's copies fit the card, else the key decodes eagerly and the run's `lm` note gives the numbers ("steps by rung"). The decode set is leased only for an admitted graph's capture and replays, never across a prefill or an eager step. A 27B LM whose weights fit a 24 GB card with too little beside them for a graph now decodes in the engine eagerly instead of Transformers' streamed loop. The pool, capture and check catches are gone: each need is judged before it runs.
