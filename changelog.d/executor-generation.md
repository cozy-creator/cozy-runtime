After a Runtime update, no request is served by an executor of the Runtime the machine ran before. A prespawn (the boot prewarm included) rebuilds its installation's SDK generation before it starts a process. A prespawned process of a replaced generation yields at its claim, and so does one started in another replica's executor slot. A warm executor of a replaced generation is retired before the next attempt enters it, and one starts from the current generation; nothing is refused.
