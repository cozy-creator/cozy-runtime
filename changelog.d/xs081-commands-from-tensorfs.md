The executor reads TensorD's commands as `tensorfs.executor`'s classes, the Rust structs TensorD writes them from: an unknown member is ignored, a missing one takes its default, a needed one fails `command_malformed` by its path. The Runtime's copies of them in `executor_commands.py` are gone, and with them `Start.ledgers`, `Invoke.max_input_bytes`, `CallInterface.native_effect` and `Activate`'s follower fields, which TensorD never sent. Requires tensorfs 0.15.1.
