Datacenter Blackwell (B200, B300) has an 8-bit sparse attention kernel, `sol-attn-fa4-fp8`: Sol's FA4 route with e4m3 exact tiles, FA4 FP8 dense rows and cuDNN for the model's exact paths. H3 at 4 or 8 bits selects it there. `sol-attn` is honestly 16-bit everywhere: its exact tiles are BF16 and its dense rows the first 16-bit kernel after it, where it used to run FP8 tiles and the site's 8-bit dense kernel at 8-bit sites. A sparse row declares the kernel its exact paths take (`exact_rows`). The Kitchen kernels need int8 tensor cores, so Blackwell Ultra (sm103) no longer offers them.
