A construction of several models now attaches the machine's GPU weights at its load (the many-model load dropped `device_weights`). With every component mapped on that card torch is held to 21.04 GB and its denoise steps run 3.3–4.5 s slower; the plane's facts now count that pressure (`alloc_retries`, `alloc_ooms`, `cache_releases`, `cache_released_bytes`).
