LLM decode holds only what it reads, and nvfp4 holds its payload once (cr-319). Qwen3.6-27B ran out of memory at decode on a 4090 and a 5090 with every weight pinned. The decode's graphs leased the whole model, its vision tower included; now they lease the language layers, the head and a draft head, and the vision tower is evictable once the prefill is done. A graph whose check finds no room beside its pool goes, and that step decodes eagerly, instead of failing the run. An nvfp4 lane kept a second copy of its 4-bit payload (8 GiB on Qwen3.6-27B): a leaf's view of its stored bytes, or a stored F4 payload read as its U8 carrier, was taken for a derived tensor. A block with a final buffer (nvfp4's swizzled block scales) now holds every target there and no stored bytes beside it, so the row-major scales are not kept twice (1.0 GiB on Qwen3.6-27B nvfp4). The out-of-memory line gives the room varena found when it refused, and says "every evictable byte offered" only when every lever was tried.
