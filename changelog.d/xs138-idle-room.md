Idle graph recording takes free room only. Before each of its ops on the device it reads the card: where the free bytes and the process's own free working pages are under 256 MiB, or under twice what the op's tensors hold, the job ends for that idle period and is tried again at a later one. Before, an idle op with no room could sit in the allocator's 300 s wait for memory outside the process, and a request that arrived meanwhile waited with it (SDXL at a 6 GiB cap: 293 s).
