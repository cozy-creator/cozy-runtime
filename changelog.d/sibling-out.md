Sibling linears write their column ranges of one output: an encoded leaf takes `out=` (`leaf(x, out=view)`, and `rows(linear)(x, start, stop, out=view)` for a row window), and its int8 or fp8 GEMM writes there in place, the mma.sync and TMA families' epilogues storing rows `ldo` apart. A kernel that reads a packed `[..., q | k | v]` buffer (Kitchen's Sol producer, packed-QKV attention) gets it with no concatenation; other leaves and fallbacks copy into the view, and an unbaked LoRA accumulates into it (`lora.accumulate` takes any output whose rows flatten). MiniMax H3's sparse step drops its Q|K|V cat (0.54 s a step on an RTX 4090, xs-089).
