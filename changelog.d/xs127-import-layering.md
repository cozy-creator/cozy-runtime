Import layering (xs-127). The torch-free layer (the CLI, author declarations, the wire protocol, `describe` and discovery) imports no torch-layer module, at the top or in a function, and imports without torch; the torch layer loaded past an executor's device boundary (residency, loop, execution, prepared, weights and the Triton-only attention modules) imports torch and each other at the top. Cycles removed: `asset_inputs` moved to `author._assets`; `Weights.read_into` reads through a reader the Runtime binds instead of importing `weights_store`; residency no longer imports `weights` (a layout says it binds encoded parts, `encoded`) or is imported by `graphs` and `accel` (`ResidencyError` and `StaleBinding` live in `accel`); `native_quantization` no longer imports `quantization` (it owns `PARAMS`, `PER_ROW`, `QuantizationStats` and the encode pool); `derive` and `internal.encoding` re-export nothing, which ends the `model_values` cycle (importing `internal.model_values` first used to fail). `derive.recipes` holds only `quantize`'s declaration; an executor installs `operations.run_recipe`. The attention `Choice` record moved to `executor_commands`.
