A weight block of several layers that the card has no room for runs from its host copy, as one linear layer already did: each of its linear layers in windows of its output rows, its other values (a norm's scale) copied to the card for its forward. A block is split to its layers, never refused for its size. One block of a model holds a host copy at a time, so a pass streaming its layers holds one layer's bytes.
