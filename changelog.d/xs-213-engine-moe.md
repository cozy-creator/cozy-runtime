CozyEngine decodes Qwen3.5-family mixtures of experts (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, xs-213): the router, the shared expert, the routed experts and their combine run inside the engine's captured step (`engine.moe`), as Transformers' `Qwen3_5MoeSparseMoeBlock` computes them. A ggml lane's routed experts are llama.cpp's MUL_MAT_ID (MMVQ with expert ids, `_ggml.matmul_ids`), each expert's blocks read through a table of addresses (one recorded patch of the vendored `mmvq.cu`), which Transformers' engine uses too, so its q8_0 decode records as a graph. The grouped bf16 and int8 expert kernels now read their weights in 16-byte vectors (an alignment hint on the pointer, not the integer address it is cast from).
