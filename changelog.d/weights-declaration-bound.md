A weights declaration is bounded by TensorFS's own document limit instead of a fixed 1 MiB (executor spool, worker writer broker, `weights_intent`) or 16 MiB (workspace journal), so a whole-model declaration such as SDXL's 1.09 MB assembly reaches its native writer. `ValidateWeightsCheckpoint` accepts the declaration by reference: omitted bytes are bound by the subject's declaration digest.
