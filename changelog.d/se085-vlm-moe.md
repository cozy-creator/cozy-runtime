`cozy_runtime.vlm` serves Qwen-VL checkpoints (Qwen3.5, Qwen3.6; dense and MoE) for any package: `AskRequest`/`AskResult`, `VisionLM`, `ask`, with `temperature`/`top_p`/`seed` (greedy at 0) and a `thinking` switch that returns only the answer. `cozy_runtime.moe` splits Transformers' fused experts into one varena block per expert (`RoutedExpert`, `Experts`) and records routing (`Routing`, LRU and next-use replay).
