int8-convrot convolutions (cr-327): an `int8.convrot` recipe may name Conv3d filters, which a lane stores channels-last, `[O, kt, kh, kw, I]` (`formats.conv_declared`: a row a (output channel, tap), rotated over the input channels by H256, exact per tap). Runtime serves them through `encoding.leaves.Int8ConvRotConvLeaf`, an int8 NDHWC implicit-GEMM conv on the int8 tensor cores (`_kernels._conv.conv8`, Cozy's `kernels/csrc/conv/conv8.cuh`): activations quantized one scale a voxel, each tap's int32 sum promoted with its scales, bias and residual in the epilogue. A package declares its conv network's residual blocks with `fusion.ConvBlocks` (`ConvResidual` with an optional `ConvModulation`, `TemporalMix`) and `fusion.install_conv_blocks` folds each GroupNorm, affine and SiLU into the quantize pass. On families whose 16-bit tensor cores beat int8 mma.sync (sm90, sm100, sm103) the filters convolve decoded. H3's latent upscaler, 15 s clip to 1440p on an RTX 5090: 1.38 s against eager bf16's 3.62 s, 66 dB against bf16.
