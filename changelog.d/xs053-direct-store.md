The Runtime reads and writes the machine's TensorFS store itself (cozytensors D10). A checkpoint opens by its header ref; configs come from its components (`component.config`) and assets from the store, so a model gets its tokenizers and processors without a file list. Private checkpoints are fetched on first read under the request's Hub capability (`Load.hub`, `Invoke.hub`). Derived weights are written with TensorFS's `Writer` straight into the store under a replay key the Runtime computes, kept as `local/derived@<key>`, shown as the run's checkpoint item, and uploaded to `Invoke.destination`; a failed upload ends `upload_failed` with the local checkpoint kept. `ModelArtifact` names its checkpoint (`checkpoint`) and replay key (`transaction`). TensorD's model-source, object-file, fetch and weights-writer channels are gone.
