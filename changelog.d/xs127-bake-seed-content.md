A LoRA bake's rounding seed names a package hook's factors by their checkpoint and component, not by the package that loaded them: a published package and the same source installed locally (or under another version) bake the same bytes. MiniMax-H3 Turbo, whose turbo LoRA is baked into the int8 pages, gave a different video for the same seed from `_/minimax-h3@0.1.7` and from its source run as `./minimax-h3` (18 dB apart, both clean). Turbo outputs for a given seed change once against 0.34.x, the new seed's rounding noise; the LoRA, its scale and the quality are unchanged.
