A managed serving call's executor starts while its weights download (h3a-087): on GPUs its root holds or that are free, at the lowest CPU priority, it imports the package, initializes CUDA and proves its attention kernels (the model's preferred chain, then the ranked table) while those cards are idle. The grant prefers those GPUs and the replica adopts the started process; the process yields when another root leases or is granted one of its GPUs. The parent run records the `Starting model executor` and `Proving attention kernels` phases beside `Downloading model weights`. Probe verdicts are kept per executor, so construction and a request's pin check no longer re-probe. Triton's cache lives in the machine kernel store. MiniMax H3 states its packed attention length at request intake (`author.expect_attention`), so Sol's per-length kernel starts building before text conditioning.
