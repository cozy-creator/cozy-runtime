A LoRA'd block that streams keeps its baked bytes in host RAM after its first bake, keyed by its resident-page identity (set, block, fill identity, overlay): a later refill of the same page copies them back through the same native region, with no store read, factor read or re-bake, byte-identical to the first bake. Process lifetime only, never on disk; within free host RAM less the larger of 8 GiB and a tenth, least recently used first; a model's pages go when its fill changes or it closes. Fill traces name such refills `baked`.
