A sharded stage runs at its group's degree or on one GPU, whichever was measured faster for it: the key is the package build, function, stage, shape class, card, degree and Runtime build. A key runs at each degree it has no steady step time for, once, and then keeps the faster; one GPU means every GPU runs the stage unsharded on its own, nothing crossing. The machine records each degree's steady step and the attention kernels it served (cr-348 C).
