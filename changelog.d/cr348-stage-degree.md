A sharded stage runs at its group's degree or on one GPU, chosen at its first sharded call by its key: the package build, function, stage, that call's video shape exactly and its other tensors (a prompt's text rows) to the next power of two, card, degree and Runtime build. One GPU is measured first and costs what one GPU does: GPU 0 runs each sharded call unsharded and its output crosses, while the other GPUs run the rest of the stage's program on it and compute, fetch and hold none of the call. The group's degree is then tried once, and a second step slower than one GPU's steady step moves the rest of that stage to one GPU. It is not tried where one GPU's last sharded call paged weights onto the card and the exchange crosses host memory. A measurement is never repeated, except one GPU's while it is cold. The machine records each degree's steady step and the attention kernels it served; each stage's row in `transport.stages` names its key, its degree and, where so, `hosted`, `trial_ms`, `paged_bytes` and `skipped_degree` (cr-348 C).
