A sharded stage runs at its group's degree or on one GPU, whichever was measured faster for it, chosen at its first sharded call: the key is the package build, function, stage, that call's video shape exactly and its other tensors (a prompt's text rows) to the next power of two, card, degree and Runtime build. A key runs at each degree it has no steady step time for and then keeps the faster; a time is recorded only from steps in which no GPU read weights from disk or saw a kernel build or land, else the next request measures again. One GPU means every GPU runs the stage unsharded on its own, nothing crossing. The machine records each degree's steady step and the attention kernels it served; each stage's row in `transport.stages` names its key (cr-348 C).
