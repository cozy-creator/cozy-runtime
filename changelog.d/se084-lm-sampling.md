`lm.generate(..., temperature=, top_p=, seed=)` samples. A token is a Gumbel-max draw: the argmax, over the top-p nucleus, of the scores over the temperature plus Gumbel noise hashed from (seed, the token's index, the vocabulary entry). It stays inside the replayed graph; a step with a sampling row records its own graph, so all-greedy steps are unchanged. A row's draws depend only on its own scores, seed and position: the same prompt and seed replay, a row draws the same tokens alone or in a batch, MTP drafting verifies draws exactly as greedy tokens, and Transformers' loop (the fallback) draws the same tokens. Temperature 0 (the default) is the plain argmax.
