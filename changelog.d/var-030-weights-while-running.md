A call holds a component's shared weights (its common block) on the card only while that component runs, or once something reads them outside its forwards, and no longer because the call declared it. A weight block that finds no room first takes back those held only for reads by components not running. A block that is one linear layer and still finds none runs from the host, in windows of its output rows copied in, as many as the card's room takes, instead of failing `memory_exhausted`. At a 6 GiB cap, LTX-2.5's denoise held its VAE's, connectors' and audio VAE's weights (1.3 GiB) beside its transformer, and its 1.44 GiB text projection had no room (var-030 §12).
