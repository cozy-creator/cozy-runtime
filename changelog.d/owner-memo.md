A rented upload no longer re-downloads a model its owner already published from another machine. The workspace reports `memo_lookup`: a completed `upload_huggingface`/`upload_civitai` records `memo.record` (call index, operation, computation digest, checkpoint) on its root execution, and under `MachineExecutionSubmit.owner_memo` the call first records `memo.lookup` while an owner wait read is open and parks until `MACHINE_EXECUTION_ACTION_ANSWER_MEMO` answers it or the owner reads past it. A valid hit completes the call from the owner's checkpoint without a source request; an empty, late, refused or absent answer computes as before. Nothing goes to Tensorhub.
