A fused DiT block's SwiGLU (`fusion.GatedMLP` with separate gate and up linears) no longer writes its product in bf16 for an int8 down projection to read back: the down linear's quantization pass computes `silu(gate) * up` itself (`kernels.int8_rows(gate, (down,), activation="swiglu", up=up)`), rounding each product as the eager kernel does, so its operand is the same. Qwen-Image 2.1's 4 MP step drops one bf16 [16384, 12288] write and read per block (xs-134).
