`VideoPreparation` declares a video's fit: `max_short_edge`, `max_long_edge`, `max_pixels`, `max_fps` and `audio` (`False`, or the name of a boolean member such as `"use_audio"`). It is the most of the input the package reads and never a refusal; a client may reduce a larger source to it before upload. Declaring any of them writes the `video-fit/1` profile (`video-trim/1`'s members plus the fit); a duration alone still writes `video-trim/1` byte for byte. A `video-fit/1` with no fit member refuses.
