A streamed decode's held blocks give way when a block it streams is refused room beside them, and from the next token it holds less by that block's bytes (cr-319). Klein's prompt upsampler (10.1 GiB int8) at a 6 GiB cap held 4.0 GiB to decode through Transformers' loop, and a 0.2 GiB layer it streams then failed `memory_exhausted` (gate 44062127052). The weights held only for speed are the first room a refusal takes.
