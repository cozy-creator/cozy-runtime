A sparse attention kernel's dense rows come from its own declaration or from the table: rows it does not declare (BF16 Sol's dense steps, protected prefix and dense paths, and any sparse kernel's exact paths without a declared kernel) run on the table's 16-bit dense ladder for the card's class, which picks its band per call, so a protected prefix and a refiner's short document take their own. The binding derived from a site's walk is gone, and so is Sol's separate exact kernel. Hopper's `sol-attn-sage2-fixed-kmean` exact paths (H3's refiner, block 1 and its key-gain blocks) move from SDPA to FlashAttention-3, and `sol-attn-fa4-fp8`'s from a declared cuDNN to the 16-bit ladder (cuDNN today). A group's followers take rank 0's 16-bit dense rows as a ladder. Only a 16-bit sparse kernel may leave its dense rows undeclared.
