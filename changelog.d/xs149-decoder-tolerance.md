Reading video and audio tolerates what real files hold, with a warning on the field: extra streams (the first is used), wide audio (mixed down to stereo), a gap in the sound (filled with silence), a clock that restarts or runs backwards, damaged packets, a wrong declared frame count, a flipped display matrix, frames that change size partway. `save_video_stream` takes any source clock: a variable frame rate, a clock that doesn't start at 0 or sound that starts late is written at the nominal rate, with the sound placed against the first frame, so SeedVR2 can upscale a phone's video, WebM, AVI and Matroska.
