The Runtime names its own work while a call waits on it (cl-921): once a phase has held the call for 1 s it shows under the stage it runs in, `encoding prompt / loading text_encoder` with `unit: "bytes"` (`position` read of `total` to read), `compiling <kernel>`, `capturing graphs`, `checking <kernel>`, `opening <kernel>`, `constructing <model>`, `starting the GPU`, `importing <package>`, `warming <package>`; when it ends the stage shows its own name again. A load or a compile is off the stage and step clocks and is its own row of the record, so a first step that streamed its weights no longer reads as the pace (fibo-2 46780: step 1 timed 247 s from the text encoder's first call). A child call's byte counts relay past 1e9.
