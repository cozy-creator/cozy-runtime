`author.denoise_loop(step, steps, state=…)`: a package declares its loop and the Runtime runs it eagerly, as one resident captured program, or as streamed stages cut at the component's own block boundaries, by what stays on the device after a real eager first step. The Runtime owns capture, pools, fallback, cancellation and the program's lifetime; each request's loops are in `execution.loops`. `author.hold_dtype` and `author.plain_forward` replace packages' use of `PLACED` and the `_varena` marker.
