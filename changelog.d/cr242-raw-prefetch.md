A LoRA-baked (hooked) block is prefetched one block ahead like any other: its stored bytes are read raw and its demand bakes them in place, so a cold model's first fill no longer reads each block serially inside the step (H3 Turbo on a cold RTX 5090: 2.9 s of demand reads in denoise step 1, cr-242). A cast block with no kept cast page prefetches its stored bytes. The run record times each demand fill's read and bake (`moved.bake`).
