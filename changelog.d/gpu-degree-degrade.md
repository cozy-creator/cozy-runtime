A model-bearing placement pinned to K devices now runs at the largest declared sequence-parallel degree d <= K (one device when none fits) instead of refusing `device_group_unsupported`; the K-d left-out devices stay free device lanes and the worker publishes a `warning` activity note. An unpinned placement on an empty wide envelope is placed as if pinned to the whole envelope, and the local owner pins the whole envelope when no declared degree fits.
