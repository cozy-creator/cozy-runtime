Float16 fills qualify their fp8-rowwise and mxfp8 lanes. The mined spec vectors now run at the producer's own logical dtype instead of being skipped for a float16-only fill, which had dropped the observed record and made `tensorfs.fit` refuse `encoding_unqualified` on every card (run 1312). mxfp8 decodes into float16 through an exact f32 product, so block scales below float16's range no longer flush to zero.
