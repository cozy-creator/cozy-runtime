An attempt's device allocation failures reach varena, never a library's silent fallback (cr-206). cuDNN ran the next heuristic engine when one's workspace did not fit and PyTorch cached it on the call thread, which outlives attempts: after a crashed H3 attempt under memory pressure, every later run on that executor kept its video but decoded different audio until the executor was replaced. On an attempt's thread the allocator's failure now raises (`internal/library_oom.py`), varena makes room and the op runs again with the engine the heuristics prefer. `tests/test_library_oom.py` reproduces it on a GPU: a starved, crashed attempt, then the next attempt against a fresh process (RTX PRO 4500: 6 of 8 convolutions differed before, none after).
