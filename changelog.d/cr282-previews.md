Live previews (cr-282): `cozy_runtime.author.preview(x0, vae)` shows a loop's latents to a client watching the run, a small WebP (an animated one for video) projected by the VAE's linear factors at most twice a second, encoded off the loop's thread. VAEs: `sdxl`, `flux2`, `qwen-image`, `qwen-image-2`, `minimax-h3`, `pixels`. Nothing is computed while no client watches. The executor's hello says `previews: 1`; TensorD's `preview` command says a client watches a call.
