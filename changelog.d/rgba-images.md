Images keep their alpha. `out.save_image` takes a PIL image of any mode: one with transparency (RGBA, LA, a keyed palette) saves as RGBA PNG or lossless WebP with its alpha exact, the rest as RGB; `ImageFrame(width, height, pixels, mode="RGBA")` does the same without Pillow. `ImageAsset.read()` composites transparency over white instead of dropping it (what lay under a clear pixel used to show), and `read(mode="RGBA")` returns the alpha. A `Shape` table's `None` entry now sizes a request that leaves its optional field unset.
