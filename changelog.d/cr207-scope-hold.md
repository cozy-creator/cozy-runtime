A component-use scope holds the components it names that have no blocks bound for the scope (cr-207). Varena binds such a component whole for each op that reads it outside its own forward; H3's turbo LoRA overlay, read by the DiT's hooks on every projection call when the LoRA cannot bake (nvfp4), was re-bound 22 million tensors a run, leaving the GPU idle most of each step. H3 1.45.2 ref2va_turbo 5 s on an RTX PRO 4500: `--bits 4` 23.0 → 11.9 s/step (`--bits 8` 12.6–13.0). The W4A4 activation amax reads the activation once (`vector_norm`, bit-identical, 3x faster).
