A CPU executor stays within its pod's `memory.max`. Its room is the cgroup's, not the host's `MemAvailable`; a step holds the prefix of a component whose float32 copies fit it (less the host headroom) and streams the rest, each block read and widened per forward; an idle block, and a block's 16-bit stored copy once widened, go back to the store. A decode that cannot bind its whole model streams through Transformers' loop. Klein's upsampler (36 GiB as float32) was killed by the kernel under a 29.8 GiB cap with every weight held twice.
