LoRAs bake into nvfp4 weights, like every other lane (cr-207). The bake rounds E2M1 stochastically on the block scales already stored, and grows a block's E4M3 scale only where the baked values pass 7/6 of its range. A fused Triton kernel does the bake, checked at build time like the other forms. On H3's turbo LoRA over 18 real DiT linears: stochastic keeps 94.5% of the delta at 30.6 dB rounding SNR; nearest keeps 0.4%; recomputing every scale keeps 100% but at 18.2 dB. H3 1.45.2 ref2va_turbo on an RTX PRO 4500: nvfp4 baked 10.7–10.9 s/step, nvfp4 per-step 11.2, int8 baked 12.0–12.6.
