Language models sample as vLLM 0.19.1 does, in one sampler every decoder shares (`cozy_runtime.sampling`): bans, then the repetition, frequency and presence penalties, then temperature, min-p, top-k (ties kept) and top-p. The penalty used to come after top-k, so Qwen-Image-2.1's t2i enhancer (presence 1.5, top-k 20) drew from the wrong tokens. `lm.generate` and `VisionLM.ask` also take `min_p`, `frequency_penalty` and `repetition_penalty`; a drafted step keeps the tokens an undrafted one draws. The qwen3 tools sample as the Qwen model cards say by default: top_k 20, presence 1.5, temperature and top_p 0.7 / 0.8 (1.0 / 0.95 thinking), a new seed each render; Ask was greedy (cr-346).
