Staged admission frees only what a measured scope needs. A component-use method whose scratch
peak was measured at the request's exact shape cell (by a completed serve, on a card with no
more room, never after that cell ran out of memory) keeps every other component that fits
beside its set plus the larger of its peak and the attempt envelope. Victims are chosen by
expected reuse: stale components first, then those this attempt already used, then the
smallest one that covers the shortfall. Unmeasured scopes still free everything. On H3's
4xH100 rank 0 this keeps the 21 GB DiT and the audio VAE resident across requests; only the
51.5 GB text encoder and the video VAE are re-staged (was 6 stages, 14.7 s, per request).
