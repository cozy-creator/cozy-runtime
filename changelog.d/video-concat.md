`Outputs.save_video_concat(videos, soundtrack)` joins H.264 videos by packet copy under one newly encoded AAC soundtrack: no picture is decoded or encoded. Sources must share codec parameters and a zero-based frame clock and begin on keyframes; otherwise it refuses `video_copy_incompatible` before the soundtrack callback runs, which receives each video's frame count. `MediaDecoder.stream_audio` also reads a video's one soundtrack without decoding its pictures.
