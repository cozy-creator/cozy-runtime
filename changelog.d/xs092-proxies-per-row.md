A caller installs each dependency export from its own row's module document. Two Apps can export from one module (minimax-h3-tools' `quantize` and qwen-image-2's `quantize_calibrated`, both in `cozy_runtime.derive.recipes`); the second App's export was left unproxied and analyzed as the real function. A row with no document still reuses an earlier row's document for its module, as an older TensorD sends one per module.
