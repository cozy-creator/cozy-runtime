`fusion.CausalConvEncoder` (cr-306): a package declares its causal-conv video encoder (its causal conv and the padding it reads, a per-frame GroupNorm, its resnet and padded-downsample blocks) and `fusion.install_encoder` runs it NDHWC in fp16, each norm, SiLU and pad one pass and each conv accumulating in fp16 with its bias and the block's residual in its epilogue, as ComfyUI runs MiniMax H3's under `--fast fp16_accumulation`. The kernels are comfy-kitchen #167's, unmodified, in the native wheels' new `_kernels._conv` (`kernels/csrc/conv/UPSTREAM.md`), each probed on the card before it serves.
