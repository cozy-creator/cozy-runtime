fp8-rowwise and mxfp8 weights decode to float again (cr-338): cr-327 put int8.convrot's conv-filter branch at the top of `RowwiseDequant.decode` and `MicroScaledDequant.decode`, and it read a `rotation_size` neither format carries, so every decode raised `KeyError('rotation_size')`. That broke every fp8 lane on a card without fp8 tensor cores (Ampere, CPU) and every int8 lane derived from an fp8 source. Both decoders are as they were before cr-327.
