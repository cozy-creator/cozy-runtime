A first call's recording overlaps its eager steps instead of waiting for them: the eager steps before a recording return queued, a recorded graph's end no longer synchronizes, and the latents' finite check reads its entry sums with its first step's, so a native run's submission waits on nothing. The eager steps' own check is read once the call waits for its result. A loop with a LoRA overlay hook (`lora.Hook`) records when no overlay is armed as it records (the base forward); one armed then refuses. The overlays armed before a keyed loop are part of its program's identity, so a program recorded under none never serves a call under one.
