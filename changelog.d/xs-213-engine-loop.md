The inference engine's step launcher (`_kernels._engine`, xs-213): a captured step graph runs as the body of a CUDA WHILE node, so one launch runs steps until a step clears its own `go` flag on the device, and each step reports its record into a ring in pinned host memory that the host reads in order, any it missed named. The varena suite's `engine-loop` case measures it and holds the engine's attention and ggml kernels captured into the body against eager.
