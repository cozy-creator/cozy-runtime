An encoded leaf run outside its model's forward launches on its weight's bytes, never on address 0 (cr-208). Since 0.30.0 a weight is a varena placeholder (no storage, pointer 0) except while its block or its model runs, and `kernels.readable`, the gateway every pointer launch takes, still looked for 0.28's stand-ins, so it bound nothing; the 8-bit leaves did not take it at all. Klein calls its modulation heads directly (grandchildren of its transformer), so `--bits 8` faulted with CUDA_ERROR_ILLEGAL_ADDRESS on every card (runs 13554–13569, 13601) and poisoned the executor. `readable` now reads a placeholder in through varena (`Placeholder._varena_read`, TensorFS 0.13.10): the tensor read holds the pin past the launch. The 8-bit leaf takes the gateway once for its op, and the Triton, FP8-attention, nvfp4 and RMSNorm+RoPE launches that already took it now bind too. `tests/test_placeholder_launch.py` runs the case on a GPU (A40: faulted before, passes after). Needs TensorFS 0.13.10.

The LM engine sizes its vocabulary buffers from the output head's `out_features`: an int8 head has no `.weight`, so Klein's int8 prompt upsampler failed with `AttributeError` since 0.28.0 (run 13694).
