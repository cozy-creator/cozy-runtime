MiniMax H3 now prefers SageAttention3 NVFP4 dense attention with Kitchen INT8 Sol on supported SM120/121 GPUs when the reviewed Kitchen Linux wheel is compatible. Turbo uses its existing four dense plus four sparse steps; regular generation uses ten dense plus twenty sparse steps. Other platforms retain their existing preference, and request-scoped attention overrides remain available. The release also restores compatible DiT fusion and removes repeated LoRA preparation work without removing LoRA support.
