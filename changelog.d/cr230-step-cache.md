Step caching (cr-230). `cozy_runtime.stepcache`: a package declares its DiT's block stack (`Stack`) and installs it in its plan; `denoise_loop(..., cache=FirstBlockCache(t) | EasyCache(t, retain, cooldown, max_hits))` reuses the stack's cached residual on forwards that would change it little (NVIDIA sol-engine's formulas), per call, never by default. Verdicts are device-side fixed-order reductions, so the same inputs and policy give the same bytes. A cached loop runs eagerly and is off across ranks; the loop record carries `cache`, and each step's `plans` line ends `cache:compute` or `cache:skip`.
