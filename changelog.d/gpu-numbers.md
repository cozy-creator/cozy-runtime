Name GPUs by the number nvidia-smi shows, never by process-group rank. Group failures read `GPU 1 failed its part of the call: …`, and the divergence code is `gpu_divergence` (the worker still accepts an older executor's `rank_divergence`). The kernel-warm detail and residency notes also use GPU numbers. The triage execution record and the `gpu.grant`/`gpu.release` events add `gpus`, one record per GPU under `gpu`, next to `ranks` and `ordinals`. Those two stay until no deployed reader needs them.
