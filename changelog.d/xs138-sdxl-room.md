A keyed `denoise_loop` records its whole call only once its `after` has run eagerly: that run picks the decode's split levels and measures its pool, whose estimate is the bytes the `after` held live (its charged peak counted frees still in flight). A first call records its rest and queues the whole call for idle. When the whole call's pool has no room beside its weights (SDXL 1024 at 6 GiB), the kept program is `before` and the steps, and `after` runs eagerly after each replay; what it evicts, the replay and the idle recording refill. SDXL on an RTX 4090: uncapped peak 11.7 → 9.6 GiB, 8 GiB warm 4.05 → 2.08 s, 6 GiB warm 4.5 → 2.4 s.
