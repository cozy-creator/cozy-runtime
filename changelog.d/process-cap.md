An executor's owner can cap its whole device footprint (`process_cap/1`: CUDA context, torch's reserved bytes and the plane's maps) with `cap_bytes` on Load, Invoke and Budget; every plane budget and torch's allocator stay inside it, so an activation over its estimate is an out-of-memory the plane recovers from instead of a full card. On a GPU that drives a display, a driver-derived or granted plane budget leaves max(512 MiB, 1/16 of the card) free. Load facts give each layout's `planned_total` and `planned_floor` with decoded copies counted, and plane facts give `reserved_bytes`, `process_bytes` and `cap_bytes`. A budget-cell `wanted` at or below -2 asks for a process cap of `-2 - wanted` bytes mid-call; the stage re-places its plane inside it at its next block. A `DeviceRoom` answer may carry `cap_bytes`, the process's cap raised into the room its owner made.
