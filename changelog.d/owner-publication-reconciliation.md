A sent checkpoint publication whose machine authorization expired is no longer invisible: the execution records `publication_unresolved` (call index, publication, destination, code) and `publication_settled` events, and reconciling progress names the publication. The owner can settle it with `MACHINE_EXECUTION_ACTION_RECONCILE_PUBLICATION`, forwarding its own read of Hub's finalization status; Runtime verifies the document against the call's frozen closure and settles committed or uncommitted, refusing while its own authority still answers. The workspace reports `owner_publication_reconciliation`.
