`lm.generate(..., draft=lm.Draft(...))` (or `with lm.drafting(draft):` around a package's call) decodes with a Qwen3.5 / Qwen3-Next checkpoint's own multi-token-prediction head: 2 drafted tokens a step, verified in one pass inside the replayed graph. A verify pass's tokens take decode's kernels and per-row arithmetic (one nvfp4 GEMV for 1-16 rows, one gated-delta recurrence kernel, plain linears row by row), so greedy tokens match plain decoding exactly on a bf16 test model; on FLUX.3 Klein's upsampler 5 of 6 nvfp4 and 3 of 6 int8 captions match, the rest part at a near-tie (one op still differs). RTX 4090, Qwen3.5-9B's head: nvfp4 9.2 → 7.0 ms/token, int8 12.3 → 10.0. The nvfp4 one-row FMA GEMV is gone (one-row decode 7% slower on a 4090); the int8 GEMV declines rows that do not fit its 48 KiB of shared memory instead of failing; LM decode sessions record again once the gated-delta kernels land.
