A release root may name a published job (`ReleaseRoot.job`, workspace `release_root_jobs`). The machine takes the job's declaration and job plan, resolves its Models (a choice, the owner's binding, the authored default or a provider source made on the machine) and lands them, then mints the job invocation and its directive. A declared `accelerator` decides the device; an undeclared job follows its locked closure (torch, torchaudio, torchvision, triton), except a composer holding no Model or weights. Weights outputs go to `model://<weights_destination>` under the owner's `publication_grant`.
