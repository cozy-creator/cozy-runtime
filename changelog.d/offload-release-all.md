Idle offload (`Map` to RAM) releases every slot's decoders, graphs and holds before any model leaves the device. A language model's engine retains its drafter's weights too, and Klein maps its drafter slot before its upsampler, so an idle Klein refused every offload ("retire graphs and model uses before idle offload"). A call placed beside it then ran out of memory, as in 0.36.0 gate A where qwen-image-2 ran after Klein.
