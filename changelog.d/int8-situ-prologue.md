`kernels.int8_rows(x, consumers, activation="situ")`: the prebuilt int8 (and fp8) quantize pass takes a gated activation, `4 tanh(g / 4) sigmoid(g) * 25 tanh(h / 25)` over a row's [hidden | gate] halves, and quantizes the half-width rows for the consumers in the same pass, as ComfyUI folds MOSS-Image's SiTU into its down projection's quantization. It is prebuilt, so a cold request serves it with no compile; the cards' probe checks it against the exact law.
