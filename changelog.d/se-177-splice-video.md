`Outputs.splice_video(source, start, end=None, window=())` returns `source` with frames `start` to `end` replaced by `window` (a `save_video_stream` event stream), and every other picture and sound packet copied byte for byte. Only the seams are encoded again: pictures from the last point before `start` that no reordered frame crosses to the first IDR from `end`, by x264 at the source's profile, level, pixel format, aspect, colour and clock, under the source's own SPS and PPS when x264 writes them byte for byte (a take this Runtime encoded, which so still joins its sibling shots by packet copy), else under ones of a free id added to the track's avcC; sound over the AAC frames within two of each seam. An empty window cuts; `start == end` inserts. The window is scaled to the source's picture, its sound converted to the source's rate and channels and fitted to its frames; a blend across a seam is the caller's. Refuses `video_copy_incompatible` unless the source is one H.264 track on a fixed frame clock, no frame hidden by an edit list, with at most one mono or stereo AAC-LC track.
