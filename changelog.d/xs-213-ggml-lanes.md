llama.cpp's lanes: ggml's block types are the `ggml.*` encodings (they were `gguf.*`), and a recipe's `QuantizationPlan(encoding="ggml.q8_0")` or `"ggml.q4_k_m"` writes them from the 16-bit commit through TensorFS's `ggml-ref/1`, llama-quantize's own bytes. `ggml.q4_k_m` gives each weight the type llama-quantize's Q4_K_M rule gives the GGUF tensor it becomes, by llama.cpp's converter's map for the checkpoint's architecture (Qwen3.5 and Qwen3.5-MoE); a weight llama-quantize keeps (a router, a one-row gate) stays at source precision, and another architecture, or a weight the map does not place, is refused by name. On sm80, sm86, sm89 and sm120 the lanes multiply on llama.cpp's own MMVQ and MMQ kernels (`_kernels._ggml`, vendored from b12466).
