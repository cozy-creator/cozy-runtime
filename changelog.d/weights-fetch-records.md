A model download reports as its own step. Each model pull writes `model fetch` records at its start (model, manifest, entrypoint, prefetch, the step it blocks) and end (bytes, moved bytes, time, rate); a call held for its callee's weights writes `model wait` records naming its step and how long it waited. The download's byte samples carry `unit: bytes` and a rate, once a second, and a prefetch narrates each hundredth instead of each tenth. Clients show the download as its own line and time the steps without it (run 1560: five references read 5m35s each for 28 s of work behind a 4m34s download).
