Hooked and cast blocks refill from the host tier and prefetch like plain ones. A block under a LoRA hook whose fill writes nothing this forward (flux-2-dev, Qwen-Image and H3 transformers without a LoRA armed) has its stored page pinned while idle and prefetched by DMA or store read; a baked page is prefetched by DMA. A block bound only from cast buffers no larger than its stored ones (an F32 VAE held in bf16) keeps its cast bytes in the tier and refills them in one DMA, with no store read, raw region or cast; a decoded block keeps its stored bytes. Before, all of them re-read the store synchronously on every refill (flux-2-dev int8 on an RTX 4090: 159 GB a request from the store, 27 of 53 s generating, with 16.8 GB of the tier pinned and unused).
