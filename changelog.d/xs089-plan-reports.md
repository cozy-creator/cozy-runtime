The `lm` and `moe` kernel plans report their outcome in the run's receipts (`plan_reports`, as `fusion` does): applied, or why the Transformers path serves (no Triton, not CUDA, kernels unavailable, compiling). A model `lm.generate` cannot serve (an encoder-decoder, an attention other than SDPA or eager) decodes through Transformers with a run warning, not only an executor log line.
