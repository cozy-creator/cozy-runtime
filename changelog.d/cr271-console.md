- What a package writes the ordinary way reaches its run: each line it `print`s is a log line, each `logging` record its logger lets through (Python's default level is WARNING) is a log line at its level, and a `warnings.warn` is a warning line. A tqdm bar, a Diffusers pipeline's own included, is the run's step progress, timed and a cancellation point like `ctx.progress`; a bar of up to 10,000 steps counts each one. At most 2,000 lines per run reach its log; the Runtime's own loggers stay in the executor's.
