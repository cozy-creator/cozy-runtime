Checkpoint uploads no longer stop at sixteen pushes in flight. From sixteen, the pushes in flight double while the rate they land at improves — each level measured as its average pushes in flight times its pushes' bytes per second of push — and hold the best level measured, within the streams TensorFS's `transfer_streams` says the machine's memory holds. One Hub thread fetches grants two windows ahead and verifies landed objects while pushes run, so a push never waits on a Hub round trip and the commit finds its objects already verified.
