A checkpoint of weights alone (no construction config, as a bare safetensors mirror is: Video-Depth-Anything, DWPose) constructs a model whose package builds its modules itself: its `Config` is empty and `absent`. A model that reads its config (`section`, `mapping`, `typed`, `as_*`: a Diffusers or Transformers class) is refused in one line, `config_absent`: "model.config: the checkpoint carries no construction config, which this model reads: upload the model with its config".
