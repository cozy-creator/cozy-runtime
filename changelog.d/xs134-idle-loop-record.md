A keyed `denoise_loop` call's whole program records in the idle period after its first call (xs-134 F), so a call after any idle gap runs natively from its start. The recording runs on copies of the first call's tensors, inside its component scope, with no request context. A command that arrives preempts it between its parts, never inside package code. A call that finds it unrecorded records the whole call before running any of it and runs it natively from its start: one plan and no eager first step (the call after a cold one recorded its rest after an eager step, with two plans). No idle period opens between a call's prepare and its invoke (`Executor.awaiting_invoke`, released by the invoke, a cancel or the trim), so a queued call never waits for idle work. A recording that cannot run outside its request, or does not fit, is ruled out for that layout, and later calls name why (`execution.loops[].eager`). A reused program's row carries its idle recording's parts (`recorded_idle_ms`). The idle period runs such a job without the per-op yield mode.
