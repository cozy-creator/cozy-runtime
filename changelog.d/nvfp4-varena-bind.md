The weight plane binds an nvfp4 tensor into its native leaf. The leaf keys its roles by the stored names (`weight`, `scale`, `scale_2`) and holds them in the buffers its kernels read (`weight_scale`, `weight_scale_2`). The bind no longer fails with `KeyError: 'scale'` (H3's nvfp4 lane, runs 13145 and 13185). A stored role no leaf reads (nvfp4's `input_scale`) is left out of the bind.
