An H3 continuation segment holds its motion context once: the previous segment's tail is the frozen head of the new window instead of extra condition rows on top of a window that regenerates it, so a 12 s segment after 56 context frames holds 345 frames, not 401 (`ContinuationPlan.held_frames`), and renders 24-27% faster. The VAE round trip darkens the frozen head by about 1.3 levels; the context now carries the predecessor's seam colour and the new frames are shifted to match it, which leaves a seam step within the neighbouring frame-to-frame steps (runs 5285, 5287).
