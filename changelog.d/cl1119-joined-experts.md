Small paging units join their parent where the model fits the card (cl-1119). Each unit is one varena region, rounded up to the card's 2 MiB pages: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's 10,496 int8 experts are 3.01 MiB each and took 4 MiB, 45.2 GiB of a 48 GB card for 35.0 GiB of weights, and a 6,000-token write ran out of memory on an RTX A6000. Where the component, joined, fits the card beside its working memory, each layer's experts page as one unit; on a smaller card each expert still pages alone, so a token streams only its own experts.
