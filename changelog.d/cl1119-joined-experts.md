Small paging units join their parent where that is what lets the model fit the card (cl-1119). Each unit is one varena region, rounded up to the card's 2 MiB pages: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's 10,496 int8 experts are 3.01 MiB each and took 4 MiB, 45.2 GiB of a 48 GB card for 35.0 GiB of weights, and a 6,000-token write ran out of memory on an RTX A6000. Where the component fits the card beside its working memory only joined, each layer's experts page as one unit; where it fits unit by unit (an 80 GB card) or not at all (a 24 GB card, a cap), each expert still pages alone, so a token streams only its own experts.
