A model's absent blocks go onto the card together once its first block's working need is measured (its next block's entry, or its first in a process that measured it), one TensorFS read at the read primitive's rate, where its forward filled them one at a time between its blocks' compute. Only when the whole model fits beside the live working memory and that need; else, and with nothing measured, its blocks fill as its forward reaches them, as before. Each request's record lists what was placed and why not (`placements`). `Memory.place(model, ahead=True, need=…)` places into free room off a request's path. A LoRA-capable model with no LoRA armed reads its pages as stored: no fill call per block, so its blocks batch and the core's fill worker can read them ahead.
