A model's absent blocks go onto the card together at its first block's entry, one TensorFS read at the read primitive's rate, where its forward filled them one at a time between its blocks' compute (`Memory.place`; with `ahead`, into free room off a request's path). A LoRA-capable model with no LoRA armed reads its pages as stored: no fill call per block, so its blocks batch, and the core's fill worker can read them ahead.
