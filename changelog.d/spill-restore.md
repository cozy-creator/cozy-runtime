Tensors an op moved to host memory under pressure come back to the GPU when the next request begins, while the device has room; and a spill first drops what is kept for speed alone (LM decode sessions, recorded graphs) instead of moving it. Left in host memory, a drafted Qwen3.5 decode on a 16 GB card read and wrote its tensors over PCIe at 3-5 GB/s and ran at 90 ms/token instead of 18 (runs 10143, 10144); with the change, twelve back-to-back drafted requests on that card decoded at 16.6-18.5 ms/token, every one faster than undrafted (25.1-25.4).
