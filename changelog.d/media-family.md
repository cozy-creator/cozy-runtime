An input whose declared media type differs from its bytes within the same family (a `.jpg` that is really a PNG, `image/jpg`, `audio/x-wav`, an MP4 with brand `mp41`/`avc1`) is used as what its bytes are, instead of being refused as `input_media_type`. Only another family (an image declared, video delivered) is refused. Bytes that no signature names keep their declaration, and the decoder decides.
